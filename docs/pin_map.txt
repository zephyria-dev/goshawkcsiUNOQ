CCI_I2C_SDA0     | 14       | 53 (through PCA9306)  | NC         | QRB2210 (Linux)
3V3              | 15       | NC                    | NC         | always-on

(*) DISP_EN and DISP_RESET are in the STM32U585 (Zephyr) domain.
    They are NOT accessible as Linux TLMM GPIOs.
    Hardware workarounds required — see DSI-DISPLAY-GUIDE.md.
